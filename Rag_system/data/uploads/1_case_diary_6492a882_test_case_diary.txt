CASE DIARY
==========
Case No: CR-2024-001
Police Station: Thiruvananthapuram Central
Investigating Officer: Sub Inspector Vijayan P. (Badge No: 4521)
Date of Entry: 11-January-2024

ENTRY 1 — 11-January-2024
--------------------------
Received FIR from complainant Rajesh Kumar at 22:45 hrs on 10-Jan-2024.
Visited scene of crime at Railway Bridge, Overbridge Road at 08:00 hrs.
Scene inspected along with Scientific Officer.
Blood stains found near the bridge railing.
One metal rod (possible murder weapon) recovered from bushes 10 metres from scene.
Rod sent to FSL Thiruvananthapuram for examination.
Post mortem conducted at Government Medical College by Dr. Anitha Nair.
Cause of death: Blunt force trauma to head.
Deceased identified as Suresh Pillai, 38 years.

ENTRY 2 — 12-January-2024
--------------------------
Visited the residence of accused Mohan Das at Pettah Junction.
Accused not found at residence. Neighbours state he left on night of 08-Jan-2024.
Obtained CCTV footage from Railway Station (Camera No. 4 and 7).
Footage reviewed — accused Mohan Das seen near bridge at 21:15 hrs on 08-Jan-2024.
Witness Priya Menon recorded statement under Section 161 CrPC.
Witness Suresh Nair (auto driver) recorded statement under Section 161 CrPC.
Look out notice issued against Mohan Das.
Informed family of deceased.

ENTRY 3 — 13-January-2024
--------------------------
Accused Mohan Das arrested at Kollam Bus Stand at 14:30 hrs.
Arrested by SI Vijayan P. and ASI Rajan K.
Accused produced before Judicial Magistrate First Class, Thiruvananthapuram.
Remand obtained — Police custody for 5 days.
Mobile phone of accused seized — sent for forensic examination.
Bank records requested from SBI Pettah Branch.
