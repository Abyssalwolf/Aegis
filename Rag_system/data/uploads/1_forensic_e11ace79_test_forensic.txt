EXAMINATION AND FINDINGS
------------------------

1. METAL ROD (MO-001)
   - Blood group detected: B Positive
   - DNA extracted from blood — Sample FSL-DNA-001
   - Hair strands found (2 nos.) — Sample FSL-HAIR-001
   - Fingerprint examination: 3 impressions — 2 partial, 1 clear
   - Clear impression matched with fingerprint of accused Mohan Das
     (comparison with specimen fingerprints provided by investigating officer)

2. BLOOD SAMPLES FROM SCENE (SOC-001 to SOC-004)
   - Blood group: B Positive
   - DNA profile matches with deceased Suresh Pillai
   - No other DNA profiles detected

3. TORN FABRIC (MO-002)
   - Blue cotton fabric
   - Matched with shirt seized from accused Mohan Das at time of arrest
   - Fibre analysis confirms same material composition

4. DNA ANALYSIS
   - DNA from rod (FSL-DNA-001) matches DNA of deceased Suresh Pillai
   - No DNA of third party detected

5. POST MORTEM FINDINGS (Dr. Anitha Nair, GMC Thiruvananthapuram)
   - Cause of death: Blunt force trauma to parietal region of skull
   - Depressed fracture of skull — consistent with iron rod MO-001
   - Time of death estimated: 21:00 hrs to 23:00 hrs on 08-January-2024

CONCLUSION
----------
1. The iron rod MO-001 is the weapon used in the commission of the offence.
2. Fingerprints on the rod match accused Mohan Das.
3. Blood on rod matches deceased Suresh Pillai.
4. Fabric MO-002 matches clothing of accused.
5. Forensic evidence strongly links accused Mohan Das to the offence.
