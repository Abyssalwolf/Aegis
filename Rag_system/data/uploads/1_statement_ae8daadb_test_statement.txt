I, Priya Menon, daughter of Govindan Menon, residing at Shop No. 4,
Railway Bridge Road, Thiruvananthapuram, do hereby state as follows:

On 08-January-2024 at around 21:30 hrs, I was closing my shop near the
Railway Bridge. I heard loud argument sounds from near the bridge.

I saw two men arguing near the bridge railing. One of them I recognised
as Mohammed Das (NOTE: inconsistency — FIR says Mohan Das) who used to
visit the tea shop near my store frequently.

The other person I do not know. The argument became violent and I saw
Mohammed Das pick up a metal rod from the ground and strike the other
person on the head. The victim fell down immediately.

I was frightened and went back inside my shop. I called 100 (police
control room) at approximately 21:45 hrs to report the incident.

I did not come out until the police arrived. I confirm that the incident
took place on the footpath near the third pillar of the Railway Bridge.

The incident occurred around 22:00 hrs (NOTE: inconsistency — FIR says 21:30 hrs).

I am giving this statement voluntarily without any force or pressure.
